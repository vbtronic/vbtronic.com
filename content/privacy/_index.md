*Last updated 2 October 2026.*

This site collects no personal data. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no cookies, and nothing loaded from another server — even the fonts are served from here.

## What is processed

- **Email.** If you write to vb&#64;vbtronic.com — directly, through a contact form or through the meeting calendar — your address and message are used only to reply. The forms just open your own email app; this site stores nothing.
- **Hosting.** The site runs on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), which may log your IP address and browser as any web server does.
- **Theme.** Your light/dark choice is kept in your browser's localStorage and never leaves it.

## Your rights

To have your emails deleted, write to <a href="mailto:vb&#64;vbtronic.com" class="contact-email-link">vb&#64;vbtronic.com</a> with the subject "Deletion request". You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, [ÚOOÚ](https://uoou.gov.cz).
