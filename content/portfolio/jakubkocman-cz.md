[jakubkocman.cz](https://jakubkocman.cz) is the website of **Jakub Kocman**, an electrician
who trades as **KM-Elektro** and works out of Hoštka, near Litoměřice. Its line is
*"Elektroinstalace rodinných domů a bytů na Litoměřicko"* — wiring for houses and flats in
that region.

Built and shipped through [Bruncsoft](https://bruncsoft.com), the studio I founded and run.
The footer of the site says so: *Vyrobeno studiem Bruncsoft*.

## What is on it

One page a visitor can read top to bottom, with the menu jumping between sections rather
than loading new pages: who he is, the work he does, where he has done it, the book on
local history he co-wrote, reviews, how far he will travel, and how to reach him. The photo
gallery of finished jobs is the one thing on its own page — there are over a hundred
pictures, and they do not belong in the middle of a page somebody is reading on a phone.

## How it was built

An electrician does not need a login, a shopping basket or a database. He needs to be found,
to look like someone worth ringing, and to be reachable — so there is no contact form,
just his phone number and email where a visitor is most likely to want them. That keeps the
site static, and a static site is fast, cheap to host and has nothing on it that can break.

Everything is hand-written, no template and no page builder, and it reads properly on a
phone — which is where most people looking for an electrician actually are.
