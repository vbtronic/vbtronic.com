## Work I Take On

Through [Bruncsoft](https://bruncsoft.com) — a small student studio, and **a brand rather than a company**. It no longer runs products of its own; it builds things for other people and finishes them.

- **Websites** — a static site is €80 and a dynamic one €200. That is the whole site, not a price per page, agreed before any work starts. Prices exclude VAT.
- **Front end and back end** — the part people touch, and the database, API and logins underneath it.
- **Applications and e-shops** — something that does a job, rather than a template you spend a month fighting.
- **Automation** — the repetitive work that eats hours a week. On your machines or on mine.
- **AI tools, and deploying AI products** — including [LiteLLM](https://bruncsoft.com/services/litellm) deployed, hosted and maintained. I will also tell you where AI does not pay off.
- **Source research and checking** — for education platforms and companies that need to know where a claim came from: finding the sources, indexing them, and checking each one, including whether it is legally usable.
- **Hosting and maintenance** — offered on everything, billed monthly, cancel any time. A site nobody maintains is broken within a year.

Everything beyond the two website prices is quoted per project, in writing, before anything begins. Full list and prices are on [bruncsoft.com](https://bruncsoft.com).

## Shipped Through Bruncsoft

- [Bruncsoft Connect](https://github.com/Bruncsoft/bruncsoft-connect) — a self-hosted integration hub that runs on the customer's own server and never talks back to us. Open source.

Client work is not listed publicly — I ask before showing anybody's project.

## Retired

During 2026 the Bruncsoft products came down. Most were half-built, or competing with companies that will always outbuild us, so keeping them up was not honest. That includes the spell checker at chcek.bruncsoft.com, the 3D print shop, and the batteries app. **Nothing was deleted and nobody lost anything** — there were no accounts and no paid plans.
