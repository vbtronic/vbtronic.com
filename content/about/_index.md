## Bio

I'm Viktor Brunclík — from Prague, Czech Republic. I love technology, programming, reading, and learning new things every day. I'm also interested in law and politics.

I learned to program mostly by building things and finding out what breaks. I build with AI every day and do not pretend otherwise; what I bring is deciding what gets built, checking it, and being the one who fixes it later. This site is one of the things I built that way — Hugo, its own CSS, no framework, deployed on every push.

I'm the founder and lead builder of [Bruncsoft](https://bruncsoft.com). **It is not a company** — one person, so far, and deliberately small. You talk to the person who writes the code.

## What I build

Three things, through Bruncsoft:

- **Websites** — front end and back end, from scratch rather than from a template. This is the one I do regularly and have finished for paying customers. Custom online shops too: a cart and an order, with payment arranged directly rather than through a payment gateway.
- **Business cards** — print-ready files you own; take them to any printer, or I arrange the printing for you.
- **Marketing** — the words and pictures that go around both.

That is the whole list. If you need something else, ask anyway — I will tell you straight whether I am the right person for it.
