> **Update, August 2026:** Bruncsoft is a brand, not a company — a small student studio. It no longer runs products of its own; it builds websites, applications, automation and AI tools for other people. The post below is left as it was written.

I founded [Bruncsoft](https://bruncsoft.com) — a software company focused on building useful tools and applications.

The goal is simple: build things that solve real problems, ship fast, and keep improving. Bruncsoft is the company behind everything I'm building now.

## What Bruncsoft Is

Bruncsoft is my software company. I'm the founder. We build web-based tools, apps, and sites — starting small and growing from there.

The company has a website at [bruncsoft.com](https://bruncsoft.com) where you can find what we're working on.

## Why I Started It

I want to build real products, not just personal projects. Having a company gives the work more structure and a clear direction. It's also a chance to learn how to run something properly from the beginning.
